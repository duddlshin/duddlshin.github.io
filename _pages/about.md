I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering](https://cee.mit.edu) (CEE) at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), working with Professor Michael F. Howland in the [Howland Lab](https://www.howlandlab.com).
My research lies at the intersection of environmental flow physics and computational science, with an emphasis on uncertainty quantification.
I couple multifidelity simulations of atmospheric flows with forward and inverse uncertainty quantification methods, drawing on both high-fidelity simulation data and field observations, to improve modeling of the atmospheric boundary layer (ABL).
By bridging physics-based theory and data with statistical inference and machine learning tools, I seek to advance our fundamental understanding of the ABL and to support critical decision-making in applications such as numerical weather prediction, climate modeling, energy systems, and urban planning.
